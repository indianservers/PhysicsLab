The isothermal 300 K circuit solves `orientation Vs = Vj + ID(R+rs)` with the device law `ID=Is expm1(Vj/(nVT))`. Terminal voltage is `VD=Vj+ID rs`; circuit current is `orientation ID`. PN, LED and Schottky use orientation +1; Zener is physically reversed with orientation −1. The graph plots signed terminal VD and ID. The cyan characteristic includes internal rs; the dashed theoretical characteristic omits rs; the dot is the circuit operating point. The graph can expand beyond 50 mA and the model never imposes a 50 mA cap.

Models are explicitly illustrative rather than measured part fits. At 1 mA, Si PN / Ge PN / red LED / Schottky have declared terminal test voltages 0.6 / 0.25 / 1.7 / 0.25 V. Ideality factors are 1.8 / 1.2 / 2 / 1.1 and internal resistances 0.5 / 1 / 5 / 0.25 Ω. Zener uses Si-like forward parameters with rs=2 Ω and a smooth empirical reverse exponential branch fitted to −5 mA at −3.3 V terminal voltage. Non-PN materials are fixed by the model; the retained PN material preference is ignored for non-PN models.
