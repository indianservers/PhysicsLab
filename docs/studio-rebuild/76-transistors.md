In the controlled-VCE test fixture, an ideal two-channel source-measure unit adjusts VCC=VCE+IC RC and VBB=VBE+IB RB, RB=10 kΩ, to enforce the selected IB and VCE. RC changes supply voltage and power while the device operating point is held. In the common-emitter load-line setup, the collector control selects VCC and the nonlinear solver finds VCE; RC then changes the operating point. The dashed load line is shown in this setup. This resolves the reference's incompatible depiction of VCC=5 V, VCE=5 V and nonzero current through a 2.2 kΩ collector resistor.

The model uses an Ebers–Moll transport form with an explicitly approximate Early multiplier: qF=exp(VBE/VT), qR=qF exp(−VCE/VT); IB=(IS/βF)(qF−1)+(IS/βR)(qR−1). This permits a closed-form VBE solution at imposed IB/VCE. Transport is IS(qF−qR)(1+VCE/VA); IC=transport−(IS/βR)(qR−1), IE=transport+(IS/βF)(qF−1). Parameters: IS=1e−14 A, βF=100, βR=2, VA=100 V, T=300 K. This is an educational approximation, not a calibrated full Gummel–Poon or 2N3904 model. Inputs IB/µA are converted to A internally.

Default controlled fixture: IB=24.1 µA, VCE=5 V, RC=2200 Ω gives IC=2.5305000005405 mA, actual gain 105.0000000224, VBE=0.67753083315 V, VCC=10.56710000119 V and VBB=0.91853083315 V. Transistor power is 0.0126688284958 W including base power. These differ intentionally from the reference numbers. With a 5 V load-line circuit, 100 µA base current and 2200 Ω, VCE=0.07223759015 V and IC=2.23989200448 mA, with both junctions forward biased (saturation).

Cutoff denotes IB=0 with modeled leakage retained and undefined IC/IB gain. Saturation means VBC>0, not a universal 0.2 V threshold. Small negative collector current near VCE=0 is reverse base injection, which is retained. Transistor power is VCE IC+VBE IB; resistor losses are IC²RC and IB²RB. The sum equals collector and base source powers. The ideal SMU can source/sink current and may require voltages outside real bench-supply or component ratings; those limits, breakdown and failure are not modeled.
