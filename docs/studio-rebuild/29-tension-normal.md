Physics: equilibrium of a lamp supported by two cables. For cable angles measured from horizontal, T_A = mg cos(theta_B)/(sin(theta_A)cos(theta_B)+sin(theta_B)cos(theta_A)); T_B is symmetric, and scale normal equals mg.

Validated direct refresh, angle and mass controls, mode dialogs, reset, zoom, six responsive viewport sizes with no horizontal overflow, and production build. Evidence: artifacts/studio-rebuild/29-tension-normal.png and src/lib/tensionStudio.ts.
