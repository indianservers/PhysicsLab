## Physical model and assumptions

V(r) = k sum(q_i/r_i) - Vref, where r_i is distance to the ith source; E(r) = k sum(q_i (r-r_i)/|r-r_i|^3); |gradient V| = |E|; U = qtest V. k = 8.99e9 SI and qtest = +1 microcoulomb. The default fixed sources are +5 microcoulombs at (-1,0,0) m, -5 at (0,-1,0), and +3 at (1.2,0.8,0). The default probe is (0,0,0.5) m. Correct outputs remain V = 17668.6344627 V, U = 0.0176686344627 J, and |gradient V| = 44809.1251349 V/m. The mockup's 12.4 V and associated readings conflict with its microcoulomb/metre source parameters and are not reproduced as fake values.

The plot shows the selected physical z slice over x,y in [-2,2] m. Vertical ordinate represents potential, not spatial z. Surface height/color are clipped at +/-100 kV; numerical measurements are unsoftened. Contours use 5 kV levels over the display range and marching triangles. At z=0, undefined source samples are excluded and source glyphs indicate their signed plot limit. The numerical singularity exclusion is r < 1e-12 m. Source spheres/glyphs are plot markers, not conducting bodies. Their base copies show x/y footprints at the diagram base, not a second set of charges.

Reference choices are zero at infinity or zero at (0,0,3 m). A constant shift changes V and U and preserves E. Scalar cancellation need not imply zero field. Playback moves an externally controlled measuring probe at 0.2 m per simulation second along x, reflecting at +/-2 m. It is not free-charge motion; sources remain fixed and there is no backreaction. Pausing preserves the return direction at a boundary.

Scientific references: [OpenStax potential calculations](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/7-3-calculations-of-electric-potential), [field from potential](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/7-4-determining-field-from-potential), [potential difference](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/7-2-electric-potential-and-potential-difference).
